Your information, handled with care

Privacy policy.

Last updated: 20 October 2026

This notice explains how NuttyWonders (“we”, “us”) handles information when you visit nuttywonders.com, place or track an order, or contact us. It describes the current site and services; if those change, we will update this notice.

Information we handle

  • Orders: name, WhatsApp/phone number, optional email, delivery address and pincode, optional delivery note, items ordered, order/payment status and related transaction references.
  • Order tracking: the order code and phone number you enter, used to find and show the matching order.
  • WhatsApp conversations: your number and messages sent to or from our shop, so we can answer questions, provide order updates and support. Message records are routinely pruned after 120 days; this does not mean all order/customer records are deleted then.
  • Basic technical data: information needed to deliver and secure the site, such as request and diagnostic data handled by our hosting/network providers. The storefront code does not include an advertising or analytics tracker.

Why we use it

We use order and contact details to accept and fulfil orders, arrange delivery, process or reconcile payment, send service messages, answer support requests, prevent misuse and meet applicable record-keeping obligations. We use order-code and phone verification to show order status. We do not currently use the storefront to build advertising profiles.

Service providers

Some information is handled by providers that make the shop work: Supabase for the shop database and admin authentication; Razorpay for online checkout and payment processing; WhatsApp/Meta for messages; and OpenRouter and the selected AI model provider when the automated WhatsApp assistant is used. The assistant may receive the message you send, up to eight recent stored message contents, and shop/catalog information to produce a reply. Do not send payment-card details or other sensitive information in a chat. These providers handle information under their own terms and privacy notices, and may process it in locations outside your state or country.

Card and other payment credentials are entered with the payment provider; NuttyWonders does not intentionally store full card details. We retain order totals and payment references/status needed to manage the order.

Storage and security

Order and customer records are held in our hosted shop database and are accessible to authorised shop administrators for operations and support. We keep records for as long as reasonably needed for the purposes above, including resolving order issues and meeting applicable legal or accounting duties, then delete or de-identify them where practicable. WhatsApp message logs are routinely pruned after 120 days. No online service can promise absolute security, but access to the shop database is restricted to the service and authorised administrators.

Your choices and requests

You can contact us to ask about, correct or request deletion of information associated with your phone number or order, or to raise a privacy concern. We may need to verify your identity and may retain information that we must keep for an order, safety, dispute or legal record. For marketing messages on WhatsApp, reply STOP; for order-service messages, contact us directly if you need help. See our Cookies & storage notice for browser storage choices.

Newsletter

We do not currently operate a newsletter signup. If you email us, we will use your message to respond; we will not add you to a mailing list unless we later offer signup and you choose it.

Children

Our shop is intended for adults placing orders. We do not knowingly ask children to provide personal information. A parent or guardian who believes a child has sent us information can contact us to request that we review it.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email Kavita@nuttywonders.com or message NuttyWonders on WhatsApp. Please do not include full payment-card details in your message.

This is a plain-language description of our current practices, not legal advice. It should be reviewed if our services, providers or applicable requirements change.
