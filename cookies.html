A little note about browser storage

Cookies & storage.

Last updated: 20 October 2026

NuttyWonders uses a small amount of browser storage for essential shop functions. The customer storefront does not currently use advertising or analytics cookies.

Your shopping bag

The storefront saves the items and quantities in your bag in your browser’s localStorage under nutty-cart. This is browser storage, not a cookie, and it helps keep your bag when you move around the shop or return later on the same browser. It remains until you clear the site’s browser data or remove the items. It is not used to identify you for advertising. If you clear it, your saved bag will be removed.

Admin sign-in cookies

The separate shop-admin area uses two first-party, HTTP-only, secure (when served over HTTPS), SameSite=Lax cookies to maintain an authorised administrator session and refresh it. The access-session cookie expires according to the authentication service’s session lifetime; the refresh cookie is configured for up to 30 days. These are necessary for admin sign-in and are not used to track shoppers.

Payment and external services

When you choose online checkout, Razorpay’s checkout service is loaded. Razorpay and other external services may use their own cookies or similar technologies under their own policies. WhatsApp opens on its own service when you choose to contact us there. We do not control those third-party technologies. See our Privacy Policy for the providers involved in orders and support.

Managing browser storage

You can remove the saved bag by clearing site data for nuttywonders.com in your browser. You can also block or clear cookies in browser settings; doing so may sign an administrator out. Because the storefront’s bag uses localStorage rather than optional tracking cookies, a cookie banner is not currently shown.

Changes and contact

If we add analytics, advertising or other non-essential tracking technologies, we will update this notice and provide any choices required for those technologies. Questions? Email Kavita@nuttywonders.com.
